1. Abstract 
This report provides an in-depth look into the development of a Hospital Management 
System, focusing on patient records, appointment scheduling, billing, and administrative 
processes. The system is designed to streamline hospital operations and improve service 
delivery. This document details the objectives, methodology, results, and conclusions of the 
project. 
2. Introduction 
2.1 Background 
Hospital Management Systems are essential tools for managing medical facilities efficiently. 
They help in maintaining patient records, handling appointments, and managing billing and 
other administrative tasks. 
2.2 Objectives 
The main objective of this project is to develop a comprehensive Hospital Management 
System that enhances the management of patient data, streamlines scheduling, and 
automates billing processes. 
2.3 Scope 
This project covers patient registration, doctor scheduling, billing automation, and report 
generation. It does not cover advanced diagnostic or treatment management features. 
3. Requirements Analysis 
3.1 Functional Requirements 
- Patient registration and management 
- Appointment scheduling 
- Billing and invoice generation 
- User authentication and role management 
3.2 Non-Functional Requirements 
- High availability and reliability 
- User-friendly interface 
- Data security and privacy 
- Scalability to accommodate more users
